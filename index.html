Actor — Model — Producer

Nicole Parke is an actor, model, and producer based in Washington, D.C., working across independent film, commercial, and industrial projects throughout the DMV area and Texas. Recent lead credits include Assigned Friend and When Death Came, alongside modeling and commercial work with studios across the region. She also produces and directs short films through her production banner, Nocopolo.
